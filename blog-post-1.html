The Ultimate Tattoo Aftercare Guide

May 15, 2026   |   Alex Thorne

Your new tattoo is more than ink; it's a medical-grade procedure that requires professional care. Here is how to ensure your investment lasts a lifetime.

When you walk out of DRAVEN, your tattoo is at its most vulnerable state. The next 14 days will determine whether your piece remains a vibrant masterpiece or fades prematurely. We use advanced recovery membranes to kickstart the healing process.

Phase 1: The First 48 Hours

The medical bandage we applied is designed to trap your body's natural healing enzymes. Keep it on for 24-48 hours unless you see significant fluid leakage. Once removed, wash the area with lukewarm water and DRAVEN's signature antibacterial wash.

Phase 2: Hydration & Protection

After the first two days, your skin will begin to feel tight. Apply a paper-thin layer of our organic healing balm. The golden rule: **Less is more**. Clogging the pores with too much ointment is the #1 cause of ink loss during healing.

What to Avoid

  • Direct sunlight for 3 weeks
  • Swimming pools and saunas
  • Picking or scratching scabs
  • Tight, abrasive clothing

Need Professional Advice?

If you notice unusual redness or swelling, contact our studio immediately. Our residents are available for post-session checkups.
