Longevity Protocol

Aftercare Process

Preserve the lifetime contrast and sharp detail of your tattoo with our clinical guides.

Phase 1: First 24 Hours

Bandage protection and initial defense against contaminants.

Keep your medical-grade bandage on for the time specified by your artist. This is the most critical period for preventing contamination and locking in ink vibrancy.

Phase 2: Cleaning & Hydration

Sterile washing guidelines and ointment application.

Use only fragrance-free, antibacterial soap. Wash with lukewarm water twice daily. Apply a thin layer of specialized aftercare ointment as recommended by your artist.
