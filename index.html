Aftercare Highlights

Proper aftercare is essential to protect your body art, prevent infection, and ensure perfect healing. Follow these initial steps to preserve the lifetime quality of your custom art.

The First 24 Hours

Bandage protection and initial defense against contaminants.

Keep your medical-grade bandage on for the time specified by your artist. This is the most critical period for preventing contamination and locking in ink vibrancy.

Cleaning Protocol

Sterile washing guidelines and ointment application.

Use only fragrance-free, antibacterial soap. Wash with lukewarm water twice daily. Pat dry—never rub—with a clean paper towel.
