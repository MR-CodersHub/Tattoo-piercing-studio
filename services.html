Healing Excellence

Aftercare Instructions

Cleaning Guide

Wash your tattoo 2-3 times daily with fragrance-free antibacterial soap. Use lukewarm water and pat dry with a clean paper towel.

  • Avoid scrubbing the area
  • Use clean hands only
  • Rinse thoroughly

Moisturizing

Apply a thin layer of professional tattoo ointment or unscented lotion. The skin should be hydrated but not saturated.

  • Apply 3-4 times daily
  • Don't over-apply
  • Use recommended products

Safety & Precautions

Protect your new art from environmental damage. Healing usually takes 2-4 weeks depending on size and placement.

  • No swimming/soaking
  • Avoid direct sunlight
  • Do not pick or scratch

If you experience excessive swelling, redness, or fever, please contact us or a medical professional immediately.

Questions

Service FAQ

How long does a tattoo session take?

Session length varies by design complexity. Small pieces take 1–2 hours. Medium works average 3–5 hours. Full sleeves and large realism pieces are typically spread across multiple sessions of 4–7 hours each.

Can I bring my own design?

Absolutely. We encourage clients to bring reference images, mood boards, and concept ideas. Our artists will then translate your vision into a fully custom wearable design tailored to your body's contours and flow.

How do I book a piercing appointment?

Piercing appointments can be booked via our contact page or by calling the studio directly. Walk-ins are accommodated when availability allows, but advance bookings receive priority scheduling and jewelry selection time.
