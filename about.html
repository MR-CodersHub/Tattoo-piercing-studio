Since 2010

A Legacy of Ink

DRAVEN was founded on the belief that body art is the ultimate form of self-expression. What started as a small underground studio has evolved into a world-class destination for premium tattooing and piercing.

Our philosophy is simple: Uncompromising artistry, medical-grade hygiene, and an environment that fosters creativity. Every artist in our collective is hand-picked for their unique vision and technical mastery.

How It Works

The DRAVEN Process

Every piece we create follows a meticulous four-stage process designed to produce flawless, lasting body art.

01

Consultation

A private 30-minute session to map your vision, discuss placement, and align on the artistic direction.

02

Custom Design

Your artist creates a fully custom sketch, refined to your specifications before a single needle touches skin.

03

The Session

Executed in our private luxury suites with clinical precision. Comfort and artistry in equal measure.

04

Aftercare

Comprehensive care kit and a follow-up check-in ensure your piece heals perfectly and lasts a lifetime.

The Space

A Sanctuary, Not a Shop

Step into a space where silence and focus reign. DRAVEN was designed from the ground up to strip away every element of the chaotic traditional studio and replace it with calm, privacy, and premium comfort.

Each private suite is acoustically isolated, climate-controlled, and stocked with medical-grade equipment. The lighting is calibrated for both artist precision and client relaxation.
