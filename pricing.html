Investment FAQ

Do you offer consultations?

Yes! We offer a complimentary 15-minute consultation to discuss your vision, placement, and provide a firm quote. Bookings can be made directly after.

Is jewelry included in piercing?

Basic medical-grade titanium jewelry is included in all piercing service prices. We have a vast collection of 14k gold and precious gemstone upgrades available for an additional cost.
